Six specialist tools

These open-ended tools expose more of the calculations. Each now includes a short worked starting point and a guide to interpreting its results.

After Signal Club · graphs and probability

K₅ and C₅ guessing simulator

Compare one hidden deal, an exact success formula, and a simulation.

After Signal Club · information theory

Graphs and communication networks

Follow fixed points through graph splits and compare the value achieved by different strategies.

Open tool →Related paper

After The Assembly · rankings and graphs

Condorcet domain archive

Inspect whole preference rankings and the graph of adjacent swaps. Begin with the nine-order example.

Open tool →New: the seven-alternative atlas →Related paper

After Counterplay · practice and hints

Nim practice table

Choose your own heaps, control the opponent’s mistakes, and inspect each move separately.

Open tool →Related paper

After Counterplay · impartial games

4 × 4 nimber explorer

Edit a board, follow a legal move, and see how the next-position values determine its nimber.

Open tool →Related paper

Separate exploration · probability

Coin-pattern races

Compare independent waiting times and find a cycle in pairwise probabilities, with simulation uncertainty.

Open tool →Related paper

The games are small mathematical models. Their research sections explain the assumptions and distinguish classical background, worked examples, and the results in the papers. All ten run locally; paper and video links remain available throughout.
