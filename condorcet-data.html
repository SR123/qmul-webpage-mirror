The mathematics

What the numbers mean

A domain is a set of rankings

The seven alternatives are labelled 1–7. A ranking lists all seven from best to worst. A domain of size 100 therefore allows 100 different rankings; the size does not count voters.

A Condorcet domain allows only rankings that keep pairwise majority preferences free of cycles, for every odd electorate whose members use those rankings.

Maximal is different from maximum

Maximal means that adding any ranking outside the domain destroys the Condorcet property. Even a domain with only four rankings can be maximal.

Unitary means that the natural order 1 ≻ 2 ≻ 3 ≻ 4 ≻ 5 ≻ 6 ≻ 7 is included. The archive classifies these domains up to isomorphism. For seven alternatives the maximum size is 100, with two archived representatives at that size.

A graph can have isolated nodes or several components. That does not contradict maximality: being connected by adjacent swaps is a different property from being impossible to extend as a Condorcet domain.
