About & contact

I am a Reader in Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London, working across mathematical logic, combinatorics, information theory, and learning.

Academic biography

Søren Riis is a Reader in Computer Science in the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London. His research includes counting principles and proof complexity, graph guessing games and network coding, Condorcet domains, and learning in impartial games.

He is associated with the Centre for Fundamentals of AI and Computational Theory and the Theory Group.
