Research profile

Mathematician and computer scientist working on mathematical logic, proof complexity, information theory, combinatorics and the foundations of learning. Recent work connects the limits of reinforcement learning, expert-level AI evaluation, original term-coding research and interactive presentations of theory with accompanying Lean-checked proofs.

Selected research contributions

Logic and proof complexity. Classified implications between the modular counting principles studied in Count(q) Does Not Imply Count(p); developed complexity-gap results for tree resolution and work on resolution with Stefan Dantchev.

Information theory and network coding. Developed graph guessing-game methods linking local information constraints to global information flow; collaborative work on guessing graphs, Boolean-network fixed points and non-Shannon information inequalities.

Combinatorics and collective choice. With Charles Leedham-Green and Klas Markström, proved that the largest Condorcet domain on eight alternatives has 224 orders and is unique up to isomorphism. Related work covers classification, heuristic search and research software.

AI evaluation and reinforcement learning. With Bei Zhou, studied AlphaZero-style learning in impartial games and distinguished champion performance from expert mastery. Further work explores multi-frame learning and mathematical models of drift and selection in LLM text ecosystems.

Interactive research and software

Interactive term-coding paper and Lean proof explorer | 2026

Original mathematical research on the boundary between polynomial-time computation and undecidability with disequality constraints. The interactive edition links the paper to explanations, formal statements, proof states and dependencies in an accompanying Lean-checked formalisation. External assumptions and the scope of verification are documented.

Interactive paper | Lean source

Gomulda AI search and learning lab | 2026

Exact graph-colouring optimisation, Monte Carlo tree search and minimax endgame search, with a separate experimental neural agent trained through self-play. Browser tools support training, checkpoints and comparisons. Based on the game invented by Cristoffer Crone.

Live project | Source

Solar Eclipse Explorer | 2026-present

Scientific computation and visualisation for the 2 August 2027 eclipse: local circumstances, totality duration, obscuration and the Sun’s position, using NASA/GSFC Besselian elements.

Live explorer | Source

Further resources include the seven-alternative Condorcet atlas and interactive tools for guessing games, impartial games, waiting-time comparisons and text-ecosystem models.
